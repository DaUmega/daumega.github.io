Minesweeper

Clear the board without hitting a mine. Numbers show how many mines touch that cell.

Mines: 10 Time: 0s Best: –

Left-click to reveal a cell. Right-click or long-press (about half a second) to flag it. Left+right click together, or double-click, a number to open its unflagged neighbours when the flags around it match the number. The first click is always safe.
